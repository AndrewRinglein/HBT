# Findings — the 2026-09-03 feature run

Recorded as they were found; not fixed silently. For Angela and Andrew.

1. **The pack was stale against content HEAD.** The content chat committed the
   priest/mage pass without shipping; four pipeline-agreement tests failed on a
   fresh checkout. Shipped as a chore commit; Mercy is a power now.
2. **Every attack row's `accuracy` was silently dropped** (90 rows — Punch −5,
   Hack −5, Long Shot +10, Lightning Bolt −20 …) for want of an AttackDef slot.
   `station.accuracy-field` gave it one; every control battle moved.
3. **The schedule stows spare weapons in item slots** (the Lion's third
   one-hander from battle 7; the Raven's knives at 20). The engine wields or
   nothing. Every stat still agrees across all twenty battles, so the stowed
   weapons carried no modifiers. The schedule's rule to settle.
4. **Hero assembly agrees with the schedule on every stat, every hero, all
   twenty battles** — level grants, specialty, the L5 pick and the enchanted
   items fold to the same numbers the kingdom's builder produced.
5. **The kite held at weapon reach and never reached a power's range.** The
   Emberwright's +3 Reach put her staff at 7; Fireball (range 6) was never
   legal in twenty battles. Switch `aiKiteHoldsAtPowerRange`, default on.
   The Alpha Team's Air Mage had the same problem with Storm (range 4).
6. **40 of 211 class powers compile; 171 are inert with named gaps**
   (`content/gen/class-power-gaps.json`). The biggest buckets: "one enemy in
   melee reach" style targets that need the attack pipeline (a power that IS
   a weapon swing), auras, stealth, "up to N" picks, temporary triggers
   (Fire Shield, Deep Cut's bleed), and hex-aimed blasts (centred on a unit
   with a named gap).
7. **Fourteen zombies no longer down a hero on every seed** once the Air Mage
   reaches her Storm and weapons hit at authored numbers; the rulings test
   fields sixteen and counts drops across the sample.
8. **The eight authored encounters were never published.** Six rows were in
   `encounters.json`; the encounter session's eight lived in its private NOTES.
   Four (`+` shaped: Supper, The Kiln, The Last Company, Rime) are transcribed
   under `authored[]`; E3 is the dungeon (skipped by ruling); E6–E8 are `-`.
   Two ids for one kind: `battle.*` (six rows) and `encounter.*` (the four, and
   GLOSSARY's own example). Both approved by policy; one should retire.
9. **The Ghoul kited from the whole of Supper** — any ranged attack made a unit
   a kiter, and the kite took its FIRST attack as the bow (Rake, reach 1), so it
   wanted adjacency and safety at once and stood six hexes off for 25 Turns.
   `fix.enemy-ai-role`: kite iff ranged ≥ melee; the bow is the longest reach.
10. **The Skeletal Archer had no bow** — its Shoot's range was null in the
    authored data although ruled 5 (handoff §5.2). Set on the row with the
    citation. Surrounded's ranged chaff now exists.
11. **A module-level arrival counter broke determinism** — the second encounter
    battle of a process rolled differently from the first. uid is read off the
    state now; a same-seed-twice test guards it.
12. **Surrounded names no hero deployment**, so the party starts on the player
    edge, seven rows from the civilians, and both die on every seed
    (27 objectiveFailed / 3 wipe in 30). The row's shape, not the runner's.
    Two Zombies and a Child is winnable and losable (58/29/13 in 100) at level 1.
13. **Supper at the battle-3 roster is a wipe on every seed** (4 heroes, levels
    1–3, against 8 zombies + 6 ghouls + the necromancer); the entry itself calls
    it "a hard one for that band". The Kiln at battle 9 splits (2 clears, 3
    wipes/caps in 5) — without the burning band, which is a gap.
14. **Rescue civilians (Supper's ten) are fielded as ordinary civilians**; the
    "2 resources per survivor" reward is the kingdom's, named as a gap on the row.
15. **Every Frost rider in the Codex was a gap until the status existed** — the
    Emberwright's Frost Staff and the Frost war-hammer applied nothing. They do
    now; every control battle moved because the Alpha Air Mage's staff frosts.
16. **`capability.power` was 15 enemies' largest gap; it is gone from the census.**
    The Lieutenant's clock and the Vampire Lord's feed are `power.gain`
    triggers; Necro Bolt carries +Power on the row.
17. **Zones of control stop a move ~7 times per standard battle** (184 stops in
    25 battles at eight zombies) but provoke almost never — the kite retreats by
    sidestep, which never provokes by ruling, and melee heroes do not retreat.
    The AoO exists; the AI does not walk into it much. A sweep question.
18. **The gash-variant fielding wipes the party with or without ZoC** — the
    fixture gash zombies out-hit four Alpha heroes; the shed-by-heal claim now
    needs 39 seeds to find one heal on a gashed hero.
19. **The Ghoul swung "Eat Corpse" at heroes.** A `kind: self` bestiary action
    (heal 5, +stats, needs corpses) compiled as an attack and was first in the
    list. Self-kind rows are gaps now (an enemy self-power lane does not exist).
20. **The schedule builder leaves Surge at 0 + specialty** while the Codex rule
    (heroes.json `rules.surge`) says "Surge always EQUALS the character level".
    The engine follows the Codex; the oracle no longer compares surge. The
    builder's to settle.
21. **With Surge live, nobody is ever starved into the free half-step at eight
    zombies** — the sidestep-fallback test fields sixteen now.
22. **Supper is decided before the Necromancer matters.** It arrives at Turn 5,
    the fight is over by Turn 6, and its Raise (radius 2, the encounter
    session's assumption — the row states none) rarely finds a body in time.
    The Ghouls, by contrast, eat: 4 corpses in one seed.
23. **The kill-switch seam did not reach auras or unit-level `abilities`** the
    way it reaches triggers; it does now (auras). A disabled id that nothing
    strips passes the gate tautologically — worth a gate-side check.
24. **A stat grant BEFORE damage breaks the preview promise.** The bestiary's
    onAttack "grant +N Strength" rows (the werewolf, the nightstalker's Out of
    the Dark) would change the number the preview computed — the engine threw
    preview/applied mismatch. They stay gaps: a pre-damage stat grant is a
    station question (Law 1), not a trigger. onHit/onDamage/onKill grants
    compile (Blight the Eye: −2 Vision, −10 Accuracy).
25. **The Eyeblights never die in Horrors** (17 hp, Luck 50, four of them) —
    The Dark Rushes In has never fired in a real battle; the party wipes by
    Turn 8 every seed at the battle-12 roster. 36–50 idle activations a
    battle are heroes that cannot see a target.
