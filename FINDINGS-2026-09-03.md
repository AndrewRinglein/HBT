# Findings — the 2026-09-03 feature run

Recorded as they were found; not fixed silently. For Angela and Andrew.

1. **The pack was stale against content HEAD.** The content chat committed the
   priest/mage pass without shipping; four pipeline-agreement tests failed on a
   fresh checkout. Shipped as a chore commit; Mercy is a power now.
2. **Every attack row's `accuracy` was silently dropped** (90 rows — Punch −5,
   Hack −5, Long Shot +10, Lightning Bolt −20 …) for want of an AttackDef slot.
   `station.accuracy-field` gave it one; every control battle moved.
3. **The schedule stows spare weapons in item slots** (the Lion's third
   one-hander from battle 7; the Raven's knives at 20). The engine wields or
   nothing. Every stat still agrees across all twenty battles, so the stowed
   weapons carried no modifiers. The schedule's rule to settle.
4. **Hero assembly agrees with the schedule on every stat, every hero, all
   twenty battles** — level grants, specialty, the L5 pick and the enchanted
   items fold to the same numbers the kingdom's builder produced.
5. **The kite held at weapon reach and never reached a power's range.** The
   Emberwright's +3 Reach put her staff at 7; Fireball (range 6) was never
   legal in twenty battles. Switch `aiKiteHoldsAtPowerRange`, default on.
   The Alpha Team's Air Mage had the same problem with Storm (range 4).
6. **40 of 211 class powers compile; 171 are inert with named gaps**
   (`content/gen/class-power-gaps.json`). The biggest buckets: "one enemy in
   melee reach" style targets that need the attack pipeline (a power that IS
   a weapon swing), auras, stealth, "up to N" picks, temporary triggers
   (Fire Shield, Deep Cut's bleed), and hex-aimed blasts (centred on a unit
   with a named gap).
7. **Fourteen zombies no longer down a hero on every seed** once the Air Mage
   reaches her Storm and weapons hit at authored numbers; the rulings test
   fields sixteen and counts drops across the sample.
8. **The eight authored encounters were never published.** Six rows were in
   `encounters.json`; the encounter session's eight lived in its private NOTES.
   Four (`+` shaped: Supper, The Kiln, The Last Company, Rime) are transcribed
   under `authored[]`; E3 is the dungeon (skipped by ruling); E6–E8 are `-`.
   Two ids for one kind: `battle.*` (six rows) and `encounter.*` (the four, and
   GLOSSARY's own example). Both approved by policy; one should retire.

   a kiter, and the kite took its FIRST attack as the bow (Rake, reach 1), so it
   wanted adjacency and safety at once and stood six hexes off for 25 Turns.
   `fix.enemy-ai-role`: kite iff ranged ≥ melee; the bow is the longest reach.
